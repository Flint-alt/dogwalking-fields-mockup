Felbridge FAQs

How long is a session?

Most sessions are 50 or 55 minutes depending on the location.

How do I find the entrance?

Use the Google Maps link or What3Words above. Postcodes often cover a large area.

How many dogs can I bring?

The basic booking is for up to 4 dogs, with options for up to 15.

Is it suitable for tiny dogs?

Some fields use deer fencing, so very small dogs or puppies may fit through. Ask us if you're unsure.
