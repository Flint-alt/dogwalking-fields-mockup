How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your field

    Choose from the list of locations. Check you've picked the right one.

  2. 2

    Book an hour

    Book online with your number of dogs, date and time. Your gate code and access details arrive by email.

  3. 3

    Drive in and enjoy

    Park inside, close the gate, and let your dog off the lead. The field's yours for the session.

Most sessions last 50 or 55 minutes depending on location. Open every day, 6am–10pm.

Facilities and safety

  • 6ft fencing and gate

    Secure perimeter fencing at most locations. Some older fields use deer fencing, so tiny dogs and puppies may fit through.

  • Park inside the field

    A hard-standing area inside the gate (normally up to 2 cars) so you can let your dog out safely. Upminster parking is outside.

  • Seating and shelter

    Fields typically have seating, picnic tables and shelters.

  • Water at most fields

    About half the locations provide water. Each field says if there's none, so bring your own on hot days.

  • Poo bins and emergency bags

    Dog waste bins on site, plus emergency poo bags.

  • Exclusive use

    Booked privately, ideal for reactive, nervous or in-training dogs.

Questions

Will I have the field to myself?

Yes. Fields are booked for private, exclusive use.

How do I get in?

Gate codes and access details are sent in your "booking accepted" email.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes, through the booking system, as long as it's at least 6 hours before your booking.

Should I arrive early?

Please don't. Wait outside until your start time so reactive dogs can leave calmly.
